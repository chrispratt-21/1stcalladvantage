Partner Setup Guide

Forward Your APFM Leads to First Call Advantage

A short, one-time setup in Gmail so that every lead referred to you through A Place for Mom reaches our team the moment it lands in your inbox. It takes about five minutes.

Before You Start

What you'll need

This setup happens inside the Gmail account that actually receives your APFM referral emails, not a personal inbox. Make sure you're logged into the right one before you begin.

1

Access to the right inbox

You'll need to be logged into the Gmail account where your APFM / A Place for Mom lead notifications currently arrive.

2

Your First Call Advantage forwarding address

This address is the same for every agency: fcaleads@inbound.compassplace.com. You'll enter it in Step 3 and double-check it in Step 7.

3

About five minutes

This is a one-time setup. Once the filter below is saved, every future APFM lead forwards automatically, with nothing more for you to do.

Walkthrough

Set up forwarding, step by step

Each step below shows exactly where to click. The red circle marks the spot.

1

Open Gmail settings

Log into the Gmail account that receives your APFM referral emails. In the top right corner, click the gear icon to open Quick settings.

2

Open all settings

The Quick settings panel slides out from the right. Click See all settings near the top.

3

Add the forwarding address

On the Forwarding and POP/IMAP tab, click Add a forwarding address. Enter fcaleads@inbound.compassplace.com, then click Next.

4

We confirm the forwarding address, not you

This is the one step that happens on our end.

Gmail requires a confirmation link to be clicked before forwarding goes live, and that link is sent to fcaleads@inbound.compassplace.com, not to your inbox. Because that's a First Call Advantage address, our team receives it and clicks to confirm it, usually within one business day. Gmail does not notify you when this happens, so we'll follow up with you directly once it's done. If you haven't heard from us within one business day of completing Step 3, reach out to your First Call Advantage contact and we'll track it down.
⏸

Wait for First Call Advantage to confirm before continuing

Gmail won't forward any mail, through the filter you're about to build or otherwise, until the address above is confirmed on our end. Once we let you know it's confirmed, go ahead and pick up with Step 5.

5

Start a new filter

Click the Filters and Blocked Addresses tab, then click Create a new filter.

6

Match APFM's sending address

In the From field, enter referrals@agingcare.com. This is the actual address APFM sends your lead notifications from. Leave the other fields blank, then click Create filter to continue.

7

Turn on forwarding for this filter

On the next screen, check the box next to Forward it to and make sure fcaleads@inbound.compassplace.com is selected in the dropdown. Then click the blue Create filter button at the bottom to save everything.

Last Check

How to confirm it's working

Once the filter is saved, go back to Settings > Forwarding and POP/IMAP. You should see "Forward a copy of incoming mail to" selected, with your First Call Advantage address showing as confirmed rather than pending. If it still says pending, the confirmation in Step 4 hasn't gone through yet, contact your First Call Advantage partner contact and we'll sort it out.

The simplest real-world test is just to wait for your next APFM referral. If a lead lands in your inbox and you don't hear from us that it came through, let your contact know so we can check on our end.

If anything on this page doesn't match what you're seeing in Gmail, reach out to your First Call Advantage contact and we'll walk through it together.
