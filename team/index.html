Common Questions

A few things owners usually want to know.

Will my families know you’re not on my staff?

We represent your agency and introduce ourselves as calling on your behalf. We work from a detailed profile of your business so families receive a knowledgeable, personalized experience. You get the notes, and you step in as soon as they’re ready to go further.

What if I already have a salesperson?

That’s a common starting point, not a reason to stay away. We are not asking to replace the person you already have. We cover the overflow, including the lead that arrives while they’re with a family, or after hours, or on a weekend, so they can stay focused on the work in front of them. If that person later leaves, we can also help cover the gap. That’s a backup plan, not the opening pitch.

What lead sources do you handle?

Any inquiry you’re getting today. That includes your website, Google, referrals, your own advertising, Caring.com, A Place for Mom, and similar channels. You don’t have to use a marketplace lead service for this to be useful. Once you’re set up, forward those leads to your dedicated intake email (we set it up with your account), and we take it from there.

What happens after you talk with a family?

You hear from us. Interested families are handed back to your team with the notes from the call. If a family needs more time, we can keep in touch on a low-pressure cadence and tell you when they’re ready, or when they’ve chosen someone else, or their situation has resolved, so nothing sits in a pile as an open question.

Are you a software company?

No. We use tools behind the scenes so you can see the leads and the notes, but what you’re buying is people: home care salespeople who make the calls for you. The offer is the team, not an app to install and run yourself.

What does it cost?

We keep pricing straightforward and tied to the work we actually do, not to a full-time salary. I’m happy to walk through it if you get in touch or call 317-785-1009, and we can see whether it even makes sense for your agency.
